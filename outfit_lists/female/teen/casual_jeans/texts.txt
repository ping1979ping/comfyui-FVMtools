# Sourcing notes — every wordmark below is a real, verifiable brand, school or
# institution mark (jeans + graphic tee is exactly the "mall brand / campus
# souvenir" case for lettering — see Decision 2 in build-outfit-set.md):
#  * AMERICAN EAGLE (American Eagle Outfitters, Inc., ticker AEO) is headquartered
#    at SouthSide Works, Pittsburgh, PA; its stretch/Ne(x)t Level denim line is a
#    top-selling teen jeans brand in the US mall channel, hence the AE tag on the
#    bottom slot too.
#  * AERIE is AEO's sister brand (same Pittsburgh, PA HQ) for everyday loungewear,
#    crop tops and basics aimed at teens/young adults.
#  * HOLLISTER CO. is an Abercrombie & Fitch Co. subsidiary, founded 2000, HQ in
#    New Albany, OH; it targets teens with a Southern California surf-lifestyle
#    look and a small embroidered seagull/wordmark on the chest.
#  * PACSUN (Pacific Sunwear) is headquartered in Anaheim, CA, and sells teen and
#    young-adult streetwear, skate and surf-graphic tees.
#  * BRANDY MELVILLE is headquartered in Santa Monica, CA; its minimal small
#    logo and one-size fit made it a dominant Gen-Z brand for crop tops/baby tees.
#  * LEVI'S (Levi Strauss & Co., San Francisco, CA, founded 1853) is the original
#    blue-jean maker; Levi Strauss and Jacob Davis patented the riveted-pocket
#    process in 1873, which is why hangtags read "SINCE 1873." The small leather
#    patch/red tab sits above the back pocket on the jeans themselves, so it is
#    placed on the bottom slot, not top.
#  * READING HIGH SCHOOL (Reading, PA, Reading School District) mascot is the
#    Red Knights, school colors red/black — this project's own established
#    regional canon (see prompt_catalog/_source/events_reading_master.psv,

#  * WILSON SCHOOL DISTRICT (West Lawn, Berks County, PA) mascot is the
#    Bulldogs — same regional canon file ("WILSON BULLDOGS" banner).
#  * VARSITY and BFF are generic prints, not brand marks; no research needed.
