# Sourcing notes — every place-name wordmark below is real and verifiable (a beach-town
# souvenir tee worn back home is the "yes" case for lettering — see Decision 1/2 in
# build-outfit-set.md). This project's home region is Reading / Berks County, PA, and
# Berks families culturally vacation at the New Jersey Shore each summer, so a shore
# souvenir tee worn as everyday wear back home is an authentic, well-sourced detail:
#  * "LBI" is the everyday abbreviation for Long Beach Island, an 18-mile barrier
#    island in Ocean County, NJ; shore shops there (Coconuts and Tropics, Making
#    Waves, etc.) sell "LBI"-branded tees, hoodies and sweatshirts as the classic
#    souvenir line for the island.
#  * Avalon and Stone Harbor sit together on Seven Mile Island in Cape May County, NJ
#    — quieter, boutique-shopping Jersey Shore towns (Dune Drive in Avalon, 96th St
#    in Stone Harbor) that sell their own town-name souvenir apparel.
#  * Point Pleasant Beach, NJ is home to Jenkinson's Boardwalk; its gift shops
#    (Aquarium Gifts, Jenkinson's South Gift Shop) sell branded souvenir T-shirts
#    referencing the boardwalk and the town.
#  * Cape May, NJ — a Victorian-era shore town and National Historic Landmark — is
#    one of the most recognized Jersey Shore town names on souvenir tees, sold along
#    its Washington Street Mall shops.
