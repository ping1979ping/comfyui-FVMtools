#  * MOUNT GAY RUM red regatta caps are handed out only to sailors who complete one of

#    skippers racing in Newport RI in the early 1970s. It is THE sailing cap.
#  * NEWPORT BERMUDA RACE (since 1906) and BLOCK ISLAND RACE WEEK are real US ocean
#    races whose dated crew tees are standard summer wear on the water.
#  * NEW YORK YACHT CLUB (est. 1844) — club burgee and name sit as small chest embroidery.
#  * SPERRY (Top-Sider, Paul Sperry, New Haven CT 1935), NAUTICA (US nautical label,
#    1983), HELLY HANSEN (Norway, 1877) and WEST MARINE (US boating retailer) are the
#    sailing-wear and chandlery marks actually worn on deck.
#  * VINEYARD VINES was founded on Martha's Vineyard MA and is the US coastal-prep mark.
#  * BOSTON WHALER is a real US boat builder; owner caps carry the wordmark.
#  * "CREW" is a generic crew-shirt print, not a brand.
